1. About Protection Bot

What this policy covers

Protection Bot (“we,” “our,” or “the Bot”) is a Telegram bot intended to assist channel and group administrators with management and protection-related settings. Available features depend on the current version of the Bot and the permissions granted to it.

This policy explains information that may be processed when you interact with the Bot or connect a Telegram channel or group.

2. Information that may be processed

Only as needed for operation

Depending on how the Bot is configured and used, it may process:

  • Telegram user IDs and basic profile details made available by Telegram.
  • Channel or group IDs, titles, and relevant administrator or connection status.
  • Settings and preferences selected by users or authorized administrators.
  • Updates or event details made available by Telegram and needed for a requested feature.
  • Limited technical logs used to troubleshoot errors, maintain reliability, or investigate misuse.

The exact information depends on the Bot’s actual code, configuration, and permissions. The Bot should collect only information needed for its real features.

3. How information may be used

Providing and maintaining the service

Information may be used to provide requested bot functions, remember administrator settings, display results where supported, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, maintain service security, and respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal information or claim that the Bot provides monitoring or protection features that it does not actually support.

4. Telegram permissions

You remain in control

Some features may require the Bot to be added to a channel or group or to receive administrator permissions. Grant only permissions necessary for your intended use. Administrators can review permissions, remove the Bot, or revoke its access through Telegram.

Security tip: Never send your Telegram login code, password, bot token, recovery code, or private key to the Bot or to anyone claiming to provide support.

5. Sharing and disclosure

No sale of personal information

Information may be processed by Telegram and by hosting or infrastructure providers used to run the Bot. It may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, to address security incidents or abuse, or when an authorized user requests an action that requires disclosure.

6. Security

Reasonable safeguards

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to server accounts, databases, and bot tokens should be restricted and protected.

7. Retention and deletion

Requests can be reviewed

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or de-identified where reasonably possible.

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of the Bot by contacting the operator below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Your choices & third-party services

Manage your access

You can stop interacting with the Bot, remove it from a channel or group, or revoke its permissions. You may contact the operator with questions about information associated with your use.

The Bot operates through Telegram and may rely on hosting or other technical providers. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, which we do not control.

9. Policy updates

Latest version published here

This policy may be updated if the Bot’s features or information practices change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.
